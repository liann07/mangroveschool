Strong roots grow in rough water.

Mangrove School is a small, hands-on school for ages 5 to 16, where children learn from the living world around them and build the steadiness to stand in it.

Book a visitSee how we teach

Why mangroves?

A mangrove stands where the tide comes and goes. Its roots hold the shore, shelter young fish, and clean the water. We want school to do the same for children: hold them steady, give them room to grow, and leave the place better than they found it.

Learning in three stages

Small classes of at most 18. Every stage mixes core subjects with fieldwork, making and service.

Seedlings

Ages 5 to 8

Reading, number sense and storytelling through play, gardening and daily outdoor time.

Roots

Ages 9 to 12

Projects in science, languages and maths, plus a term-long study of a local habitat.

Canopy

Ages 13 to 16

Exam preparation, independent research and community placements that lead to real outcomes.

A day at Mangrove

  • 8:30Arrival and circle: plans, news and a quiet minute.
  • 9:00Core lessons in maths, language and science.
  • 11:30Lunch cooked from the school garden and shared at long tables.
  • 12:30Project time: fieldwork, workshops, art and music.
  • 15:00Clubs, homework help and pick-up.

Small by design

No class larger than 18, so every teacher knows every child.

Outside often

At least one lesson a day happens outdoors, in any weather.

Part of the town

Students volunteer each term with local shoreline and food projects.

Come and see us

Tours run Tuesday and Thursday mornings. Tell us a little about your child and we will get back to you within two school days.
